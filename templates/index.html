      RSI Prevention Tips

      General, evidence-informed habits for programmers — not a substitute for medical advice.

      ⏱

      Take breaks every 45–60 minutes

      Stand up, walk, and shake out your hands. Short, frequent breaks matter more than one long break.

      🪑

      Fix your posture

      Keep wrists neutral (not bent up/down), elbows near 90°, and screen at eye level.

      🤸

      Stretch daily

      Simple wrist, forearm, neck, and shoulder stretches reduce cumulative strain.

      ⌨️

      Consider an ergonomic keyboard

      Split or tented keyboards can reduce wrist deviation during long typing sessions.

      😴

      Sleep 7–8 hours

      Poor sleep is linked to slower tissue recovery and higher pain sensitivity.

      🩺

      Don't ignore persistent pain

      If pain lasts more than a couple of weeks or worsens, see a physiotherapist or doctor.
